Sign-in that enterprises trust and users don't notice

I led the replacement of a legacy, multi-tenant sign-in system with a new auth framework, rolled out organisation by organisation behind a per-tenant switch. Before that, we hardened the old one: expiring tokens, central signing and key rotation.

# in practice
  • Enterprise customers sign in through their own identity provider and can require 2FA for everyone.
  • Sign-in links in emails still work after Slack or Outlook previews them.
  • Tokens expire and keys rotate on a schedule, with no forced logouts.
  • Services can verify tokens themselves against published keys, ready for service-to-service auth.
