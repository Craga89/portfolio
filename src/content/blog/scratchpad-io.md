[scratchpad.io](http://scratchpad.io/) is a great little web app by [Sandbox Labs](http://www.sandboxlabs.co/) that renders your CSS and HTML in real time as you type, and includes [localStorage](http://diveintohtml5.info/storage.html)persistence to stop you from accidentally F5′ing it away! Great for testing out the beginnings of a design, [check it out](http://scratchpad.io/)!
