qTip2 is now officially available through the [jsDelivr CDN](http://www.jsdelivr.com/), and is the CDN of choice for the library starting from today's update! jsDelivr has better performance and availabilty than current the CDNJS service, so it's a welcome addition to the libraries availablity.

You can grab the latest stable releases (and older releases too!) from the [project page](http://www.jsdelivr.com/#!qtip2) and [official download page](http://qtip2.com/download).

**Note:** qTip2 will still be available as usual from the [CDNJS](http://cdnjs.com/) CloudFlare service, but pull request responses are a little slower than jsDelivr, so new releases may not be immediately available.
