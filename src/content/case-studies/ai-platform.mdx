<InPractice>
- AI features are built as agents and workflows in TypeScript, so any engineer on the team can work on them.
- The AI service has its own account, with its own permissions and its own bill, separate from the core platform.
- Changes to the AI service are checked by its eval suite in CI before they reach customers.
</InPractice>
